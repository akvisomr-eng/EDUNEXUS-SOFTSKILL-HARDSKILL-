# EDUNEXUS Knowledge & Curriculum Registry v1.0

**Status:** curated source register and curriculum mapping proposal; not a claim that every source has already been ingested into the live AI index.  
**Review date:** 2026-10-15  
**Purpose:** provide a source-grounded starting corpus for course, module, lesson, practice, rubric and AI mentor design. Summaries below are EDUNEXUS synthesis; consult the original sources for full definitions and revisions.

## Integration rules

1. Use frameworks as references, not as substitutes for Indonesian law, national curriculum decisions, institutional policy or professional accreditation.
2. Store source title, publisher, canonical URL, access/review date, license notes, mapped competencies, reviewer and version with each knowledge item.
3. Do not scrape or republish entire books, courseware, reports or paid learning materials. Prefer short original summaries, factual mappings, and links to the source.
4. Label evidence: **official framework**, **research/report**, **practice example**, or **EDUNEXUS-authored activity**. Distinguish automated formative feedback from human-reviewed or institution-verified achievement.
5. AI mentor answers should state source provenance, separate source facts from recommendations, note uncertainty, and suggest verification for consequential decisions.
6. Never present “left-brain/right-brain learner types” as a validated classification. Teach measurable processes instead: analytical reasoning, creative ideation, planning, attention management, metacognition, collaboration and reflection.

## Curriculum map: source → school → course → module → lesson → practice → evidence

This is the recommended initial taxonomy. Each course must eventually be represented in normalized database records, not only in this document.

### A. Cognitive fitness and learning science
- **Course:** Penalaran, Kreativitas, dan Metakognisi
- **Modules:** analytical reasoning; evidence and bias; creative divergence/convergence; problem framing; planning and prioritization; retrieval practice and spaced review; reflection and strategy adjustment.
- **Lessons:** distinguish claim/evidence/assumption; compare competing explanations; generate alternatives before selecting; define constraints; test a small intervention; retrieve from memory; plan the next learning cycle.
- **Practice:** case analysis, counterargument, design challenge, one-week experiment, learning journal.
- **Evidence/rubric:** clarity of reasoning, evidence quality, alternatives considered, feasibility, reflection quality. No IQ/brain-side diagnosis.
- **Source alignment:** OECD Learning Compass; LifeComp; WEF skills outlook.

### B. Human and social excellence
- **Course:** Komunikasi, Kolaborasi, dan Kecerdasan Sosial
- **Modules:** active listening; clear writing and speaking; feedback; conflict navigation; empathy and perspective-taking; inclusive teamwork; leadership and accountability.
- **Practice:** difficult-conversation role-play, peer review, team decision simulation.
- **Evidence/rubric:** observable behavior, clarity, respectful disagreement, shared contribution, follow-through.
- **Source alignment:** LifeComp; NACE career readiness; OECD agency/co-agency.

### C. Digital, data and AI intelligence
- **Course:** Literasi Digital, Data, dan AI yang Bertanggung Jawab
- **Modules:** information evaluation; data fundamentals; AI capabilities and limits; prompting and iterative work; privacy/security; bias and fairness; citation and verification; AI-assisted project design.
- **Progression:** understand → apply → create, adapted from UNESCO's student framework.
- **Practice:** verify AI-generated claims against sources; classify sensitive data; compare outputs; build a small, human-reviewed AI-supported artifact.
- **Evidence/rubric:** source quality, verification trail, privacy choices, explanation of limitations, useful artifact.
- **Source alignment:** UNESCO AI framework for students; UNESCO AI framework for teachers for educator-facing pathways.

### D. Creativity, design and innovation
- **Course:** Design Thinking dan Inovasi Berbasis Bukti
- **Modules:** user/problem discovery; framing; ideation; selection criteria; prototyping; feedback; iteration; ethical and accessible design.
- **Practice:** redesign a service with budget/time constraints, test prototype with representative users, record revisions.
- **Evidence/rubric:** problem fit, diversity of ideas, test quality, responsiveness to feedback, accessibility.

### E. Business and financial capability
- **Course:** Kewirausahaan, Keuangan Pribadi, dan Pengambilan Keputusan
- **Modules:** customer discovery; value proposition; unit economics; budgeting/cash flow; risk; ethical marketing; basic financial literacy; experiment design.
- **Practice:** draft a one-page business model, build a transparent budget, compare scenarios and assumptions.
- **Evidence/rubric:** arithmetic accuracy, assumptions disclosed, risk awareness, customer evidence, ethical reasoning.
- **Guardrail:** educational content only; not personalized investment, tax or legal advice.

### F. Technical and professional mastery
- **Course families:** software and automation; cybersecurity fundamentals; data analysis; operations and project delivery; communication and service operations; domain-specific technical tracks.
- **Module template:** prerequisite → concept → worked example → guided practice → independent challenge → quality/security checklist → reflection.
- **Evidence/rubric:** correctness, reproducibility, safety, quality, documentation and practical transfer. Every technical track needs an owner and review date.

### G. Career, portfolio and employability
- **Course:** Dari Keterampilan ke Portofolio
- **Modules:** skills inventory; evidence capture; CV/profile; interview; collaboration; professional conduct; career experiments; continuous upskilling.
- **Practice:** portfolio case study, mock interview, peer feedback, role-specific skills gap analysis.
- **Evidence/rubric:** artifact authenticity, role relevance, clear contribution, measurable outcomes, reflection.
- **Source alignment:** NACE career readiness; WEF Future of Jobs (trend context, not deterministic forecasts).

### H. Ethics, sustainability and global citizenship
- **Course:** Etika, Dampak Sosial, dan Keputusan Bertanggung Jawab
- **Modules:** stakeholder mapping; trade-offs; privacy and consent; fairness and inclusion; sustainability; misinformation; accountable action.
- **Practice:** scenario analysis with conflicting values, impact assessment, decision memo with uncertainty and mitigation.
- **Evidence/rubric:** stakeholder breadth, quality of trade-off reasoning, harm mitigation, accountability.

## Common lesson record contract

Each lesson should include:
- lesson_id, module_id, title, learning_objectives[], prerequisites[]
- estimated_minutes, difficulty, language, content_summary, worked_example
- practice_prompt, submission_schema, rubric_id, feedback_policy
- source_ids[], license_notes, review_status, reviewed_at, version
- accessibility notes and adaptations for different contexts
- measurable success criteria and a next-step recommendation

Each practice attempt should capture (subject to privacy/retention policy):
- learner and task identifiers, timestamps, response/evidence references, rubric version
- feedback type (automated formative / mentor reviewed / institution verified)
- score only when a rubric justifies it; criteria-level feedback and uncertainty
- consent and visibility rules; avoid collecting sensitive personal data without necessity.

## Multi-agent knowledge workflow

1. **Source Curator:** register authoritative source, provenance, version and license notes.
2. **Curriculum Mapper:** map source concepts to competencies, schools, courses, modules and lessons.
3. **Instructional Designer:** turn mapped objectives into lesson sequence and accessible practice.
4. **Assessment Designer:** define observable criteria, rubric and acceptable evidence.
5. **Fact Checker:** validate claims against cited sources and mark uncertainty.
6. **Safety & Inclusion Reviewer:** check privacy, bias, accessibility, age/context appropriateness and high-stakes risks.
7. **Knowledge Librarian:** approve, version, index and schedule review.
8. **Learner Mentor:** provide personalized next steps based on learner goals and consented evidence; never invent credentials or imply human certification.

No source is considered “integrated into live multi-agent retrieval” until its source record and reviewed content are actually present in the production knowledge index and a retrieval test returns the source citation. This registry is the curated starting point for that process, not proof of ingestion.

## Acceptance criteria for the first content release

- At least one reviewed source record per course family.
- Every lesson has objectives, practice, rubric, provenance and review status.
- AI responses cite relevant source records and clearly label original recommendations.
- Search/retrieval test verifies source IDs, URLs and version metadata.
- Browser test verifies a learner can open the module, complete practice and see evidence/history.
- No copyrighted full-course text copied without permission; no “brain type” or guaranteed-employment claims.
